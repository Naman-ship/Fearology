Folklore Notice

Many versions of the Bloody Mary legend describe a mirror ritual. These stories are part of folklore and popular culture. There is no verified evidence that supernatural events occur as described.

04 • VARIATIONS AROUND THE WORLD

Different cultures tell different versions of the Bloody Mary legend. Some describe a vengeful spirit, while others believe she is connected to ancient folklore, mirrors, or supernatural warnings passed down through generations.

05 • MOVIES & POP CULTURE

Bloody Mary has appeared in horror films, television series, novels, games, and internet culture, making her one of the world's most recognizable urban legends.
